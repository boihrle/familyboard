FAQs

Do the boards come with the words?

No. Boards come blank, and the word magnets are sold separately as packs: Chores Word Pack, Day Word Pack, Custom Words, and Emoji Pack.

Will it stick to my fridge?

Yes. The boards are magnetic and sit straight on a fridge or any steel surface.

Can I write on the boards?

Yes. You can write on any of our boards with a dry-erase pen, use the word tiles, or mix both.

Which pens should I use?

Use our Black Pen or Coloured Pen 8 Pack. They're mini dry-erase pens that wipe clean.

Can I put my kids' names on it?

Yes. Custom Words lets you choose 16 words, such as names, and we print them as word tiles.
