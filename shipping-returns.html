Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We ship within New Zealand only.

We send orders by NZ Post.

Delivery cost is calculated at checkout and follows NZ Post rates by weight.

We dispatch Monday to Friday from Auckland. Orders placed before midday go out the same day; later orders go the next working day. North Island deliveries are usually overnight; South Island usually 2–3 days.

We'll send you tracking where it's available.

Change of Mind Returns

Changed your mind? Contact us within 7 days of purchase to arrange a return, and send the item back within 14 days of purchase.

Items must be unused and in their original condition.

You pay the return postage, unless the item is faulty.

To start a return, email contact@familyboards.co.nz with your order number.

Custom Words

Custom Words are printed just for you.

We can't take them back for change of mind.

If they arrive faulty, see below.

Faulty or Damaged Items

If something arrives faulty or damaged, email contact@familyboards.co.nz.

Include your order number and a photo.

We'll repair, replace or refund it.

Your Rights

Nothing on this page affects your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.
