Privacy

About This Policy

This policy explains how FamilyBoards handles your personal information under the Privacy Act.

What We Collect

When you order, we collect:

  • your name
  • your delivery address
  • your email address
  • any custom words you choose

How We Use It

We use your information only to make, send and support your order, and to reply to you.

We give NZ Post your name and delivery address so they can deliver your order.

We don't sell your information.

Payments

Payments will be handled by Stripe.

We never see or store your card details.

How Long We Keep It

We keep order details only as long as we need them for your order and any follow-up.

You can email us any time to ask us to delete them.

See or Correct Your Information

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you.

Email contact@familyboards.co.nz.

Cookies and Your Browser

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Our site saves your cart in your browser (local storage), so it's still there when you come back.

When you place an order, we keep it in your browser for that tab only, so we can show your order confirmation.

Both stay on your device. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data.

Our site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address. This doesn't set cookies.

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy? Email contact@familyboards.co.nz.
